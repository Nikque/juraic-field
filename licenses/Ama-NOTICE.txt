Parts of the versus AI (the folder ai/) follow Ama (https://github.com/citrus610/ama, MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2023 citrus610; its licence is Ama-LICENSE.txt in this folder):

- ai/build.h, Builder::queuePair: the table of the six fixed sequences of pairs (CHOICE) is Ama's. The levels
  below the strongest search with it; the strongest level searches with sequences of this program's own.
- ai/build.h, buildValue: the points of a trial drop (the waves of the chain, less the pieces that must be
  added, plus the height of the place where it starts) and the terms for valleys and hills are put together
  as in Ama's evaluation. Of the numbers, 1000 a wave and 289 a row of height are Ama's; the others were
  measured for this program.
- ai/detect.h: trial drops are the "quiet" search of takapt and Ama, written anew for this engine.
- ai/build.h: the beam search over several made-up sequences of pairs, adding up the largest chain found
  below each first placement, is the method of Ama's beam search (which names takapt for it). Of its sizes,
  250 wide and 16 deep are Ama's, and so are the six sequences of the levels below the strongest (the strongest
  searches eight). The code is written anew.
